About SAMCO STAR

One partner. Complete electromechanical solutions.

SAMCO STAR Electromechanical Works Contracting L.L.C. is a Dubai-based engineering and contracting company delivering air conditioning, ventilation, air filtration, solar energy, electrical and complete MEP works across the UAE. Part of the Samra Group, we design, supply, install, service and repair systems for warehouses, offices and residential projects alike, backed by an experienced, qualified workforce, international quality standards and fair, competitive pricing across the UAE.
